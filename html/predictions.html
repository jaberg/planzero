Predictions

Prediction challenges for probabilistic models of Canada's future emissions.

NIR Year-Out, Last-Year (YOLY)

NIR Year-Out, Last-Year (YOLY) challenges are about predicting, by mid-april of a given year, the previous year's emissions. The challenges are named according to the year of the NIR-publication, which will be the following year, in April. So for example: YOLY-2025 corresponds to NIR-2025, published in 2025; it is about predicting 2023 emissions on the basis of data published before April 2024. Evaluation scores are theoretically available for YOLY-X challenges after NIR-X reports are published. There will be some delay in updating PlanZero to reflect the most-recently-published data.

Pathway to Net-Zero (PNZ)

Pathway to Net-Zero (PNZ) challenges are about hypothesizing pathways to net-zero as extensions to historical trends. The challenges are named according to the first year of achieving net-zero. For example: PNZ-2050 is about pathways to net-zero by year 2050, PNZ-2075 is about pathways to net-zero by year 2075. These predictions are supposed to be implemented by adding a speculative observation that national emissions will be zero at a future date, and using {{siteref("Inference Algorithm", "inference algorithms")|safe}} to generate explanations for how it could be possible.
